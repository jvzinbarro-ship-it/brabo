BRABO Studio

BRABO Studio is a personal desktop application (Windows) that creates short educational videos and publishes them to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok).

The app is used only by its owner. It connects to TikTok through the official Login Kit and Content Posting API so the owner can upload his own videos to his own TikTok account.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/jvzinbarro-ship-it/brabo.
