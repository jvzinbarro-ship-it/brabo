Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What data we use

When the owner connects a TikTok account, BRABO Studio receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and avatar) through the TikTok Login Kit. This is used only to show which account is connected and to upload videos the owner chooses to publish via the Content Posting API.

Storage

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted. No data is sent to our own servers, sold or shared with third parties.

Other users

BRABO Studio does not collect data from other TikTok users or viewers.

Deletion

Disconnecting the account in the app deletes the stored token. Access can also be revoked in TikTok settings at any time.
